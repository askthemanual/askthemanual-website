Common questions

How do I import a manual?

Download the PDF to your Android device, then follow the How to Use guide.

AI Search isn't ready yet

Allow the preparation process to finish. It only needs to run once for each manual.

The search didn't find what I expected

Try the manual's terminology, a related term, or a more specific phrase.

The page numbers don't match

Use the Lead pages setting to align PDF page numbers with the page numbers printed in your manual.
