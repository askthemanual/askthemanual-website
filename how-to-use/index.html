How to use Ask the Manual

From downloading your PDF to finding the right source page.

Your manual stays on your device.

Your PDF contents, searches, search results and personal information are not uploaded to Ask the Manual or sent to Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics.

1

Download your manual

Save the PDF manual you want to use to your Android device. Ask the Manual does not supply or bundle manufacturers' manuals.

2

Import the PDF

Choose an available manual slot and select the PDF from your device. The manual is processed locally.

Tips for better searches

Start with terminology you expect the manual to use. If there isn't a close match, try a related term or a more specific phrase. Single-word searches can naturally return broader results.
